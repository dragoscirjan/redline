# Initiative

Agree on the problem and desired outcome before writing a brief. Start from research, a direct request, or both. Prior research is optional.

## 1. Recover the context

Read supplied references and earlier decisions. If expected research is unavailable, ask for it or identify the gap. Extract findings, evidence limits, and confirmed decisions. Treat research recommendations as proposals until the user accepts them.

Restate a direct request and investigate missing facts within scope. Ask the user only for choices or information you cannot inspect. Reuse confirmed answers. Continue when the starting point and evidence limits are explicit; pause for investigation if a missing fact prevents agreement.

## 2. Agree on the initiative

Use the required interview to settle the problem, affected people, current conditions, reasons for change, desired outcome, success and failure measures, scope, exclusions, constraints, and assumptions. Alternate questions with investigation as needed.

Continue only after the user confirms these points. Existing confirmations count. Report material unresolved decisions as blockers rather than drafting around them. Keep technical design and implementation details for later specifications and tasks.

## 3. Choose the agreement record

After agreement, resolve its destination and format from the request and applicable `AGENTS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Ask about either if unclear. Confirm whether to create or update the chosen issue, document, or other record. Continue when that exact destination and operation are authorized.

## 4. Save, verify, and stop

Record the agreed points, starting context and references, acknowledged uncertainties, draft or agreed status with evidence, and next authorized action. Distinguish evidence, assumptions, and proposals from confirmed decisions.

Save the record and verify its content. Report its actual path or URL, format, and agreement status. If access or approval blocks saving, return the prepared content marked "not saved" and explain what is needed. Say when a save cannot be verified.

Stop at this handoff. Initiative agreement authorizes neither delegation nor implementation, and publication remains limited to the agreed destination and applicable access rules.
